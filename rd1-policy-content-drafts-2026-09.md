## Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy

**Purpose.** Recruitment Direct UK has a zero-tolerance approach to
bribery and corruption in all its dealings, in line with the Bribery Act
2010.

**Scope.** This policy applies to all directors, employees, workers and
anyone acting on behalf of Recruitment Direct UK, including in dealings
with clients, candidates and suppliers.

**What is prohibited.** Employees and workers must not offer, give,
request or accept any bribe, kickback, or improper payment intended to
influence a business decision or gain an unfair advantage. This includes
facilitation payments and disguised commissions.

**Gifts and hospitality.** Reasonable and proportionate gifts or
hospitality may be given or received only where they are transparent,
occasional, and would not reasonably be seen as an attempt to influence a
decision. Anything above [confirm a threshold, e.g. £50] must be declared
to [named role].

**Reporting concerns.** Any suspected bribery or corruption should be
reported to [named responsible officer] or via the Whistleblowing Policy.

**Responsible officer.** [Name / role] is responsible for this policy and
reviews it [annually].

## Whistleblowing Policy

**Purpose.** This policy sets out how staff and workers can raise
concerns about malpractice, illegality, or wrongdoing at Recruitment
Direct UK, safely and without fear of reprisal, in line with the Public
Interest Disclosure Act 1998.

**What can be reported.** Concerns about criminal activity, breaches of
legal or regulatory obligations, danger to health and safety, financial
malpractice, or deliberate concealment of any of the above.

**How to report.** Concerns can be raised with [line manager / named
officer], or, where that isn't appropriate, directly with [independent
contact, e.g. a director or an external reporting line]. Reports can be
made in writing or verbally and, where requested, will be treated
confidentially.

**Protection.** Recruitment Direct UK will not tolerate any detrimental
treatment of someone who raises a genuine concern in good faith, whether
or not it turns out to be substantiated.

**What happens next.** Concerns are acknowledged within [confirm, e.g. 5
working days], investigated proportionately, and the outcome — so far as
confidentiality allows — is fed back to the person who raised it.

## Conflict of Interest Policy

**Purpose.** To ensure that personal, financial, or other interests never
compromise the fairness and integrity of our recruitment services to
candidates and clients.

**What counts as a conflict.** Situations where an employee, worker, or
director (or someone close to them) has a personal or financial interest
in a placement, client, or supplier decision that could influence, or
appear to influence, their judgement.

**Declaration.** Anyone identifying a potential conflict must declare it
to [named role] as soon as it arises, before taking any further action on
the matter concerned.

**Management.** Declared conflicts are recorded and managed on a
case-by-case basis — which may include removing the individual from the
decision, or having a second person review the outcome.

## Agency Workers Regulations (AWR) Compliance Statement

**Purpose.** This statement confirms how Recruitment Direct UK applies
the Agency Workers Regulations 2010 to temporary workers it supplies.

**Day-one rights.** From the first day of an assignment, agency workers
are entitled to the same access to collective facilities and amenities
(e.g. staff canteen, car parking) as comparable permanent employees of
the hirer, and to information about relevant vacancies with the hirer.

**Equal treatment after 12 weeks.** Once an agency worker has completed
12 calendar weeks in the same role with the same hirer, they become
entitled to the same basic working and employment conditions — including
pay, working time, and annual leave — as if they had been recruited
directly by the hirer for that role.

**How we manage this.** Recruitment Direct UK tracks qualifying periods
for each placement and works with clients to confirm comparator pay and
conditions ahead of the 12-week point, so entitlements are applied
correctly and on time.

## Right to Work & Immigration Compliance Policy

**Purpose.** Recruitment Direct UK verifies the right to work in the UK
for every candidate before placement, in line with the Immigration,
Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 and Home Office guidance.

**Process.** Before any placement, we check and retain evidence of each
candidate's right to work — either original documents or a Home Office
online right-to-work check — and record the check date and outcome.

**Ongoing checks.** Where a candidate holds time-limited permission to
work, a follow-up check is scheduled ahead of expiry to confirm continued
eligibility for the assignment.

**Non-compliance.** No candidate is placed into an assignment without a
satisfactory right-to-work check, and any concern identified during a
check is escalated to [named role] before proceeding.

## IR35 / Off-Payroll Working Compliance Statement

**Purpose.** This statement explains how Recruitment Direct UK supports
clients with off-payroll working (IR35) compliance for contract
placements engaged through personal service companies or other
intermediaries.

**Status Determination Statements.** Where the off-payroll working rules
apply, Recruitment Direct UK requires the client (as the end hirer) to
issue a Status Determination Statement (SDS) for each relevant engagement
before it begins, and passes this down the labour supply chain as
required by HMRC guidance.

**Our role.** As the fee-payer in the supply chain, Recruitment Direct UK
applies the correct tax and National Insurance treatment based on the
client's determination, and maintains records of each SDS received.

**Disagreements.** Where a worker disagrees with their status
determination, we support the client's statutory disagreement process
and will not act until it is resolved.

getting a number wrong is worse than not having the page yet.

employees — worth confirming it's in place today even before deciding
