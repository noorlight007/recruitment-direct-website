Common questions

Hiring staff through Recruitment Direct

How quickly can you find me staff? +

Enquiries submitted through AI Hire Now are picked up straight away, with AI-screened candidates matched against your brief and recruiter verified before they ever reach you. WhatsApp and email enquiries go to a consultant for a fast personal response.

Which sectors do you cover? +

Twenty sectors in total — from Construction, Logistics and Healthcare through to IT & Technology, Legal and Property. See the full list above, or search for your role directly.

Can I hire just one person, or is this for bulk staffing only? +

Both. Requests range from a single role to large multi-person orders — recent placements include everything from one facilities manager to 24 labourers in a single enquiry.

Do you supply staff across the whole UK? +

Yes — Recruitment Direct has placed staff UK-wide since 2006, with dedicated coverage across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

Is there a cost to submit an enquiry? +

No — submitting your staffing requirement, by any method, is free. You'll only discuss terms once we're matching candidates to your brief.

What is AI Hire Now? +

AI Hire Now takes your enquiry and creates a live job directly in our system, starting the matching process immediately — no waiting on a callback to get the ball rolling.
